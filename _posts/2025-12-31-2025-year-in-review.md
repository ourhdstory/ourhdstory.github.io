2025 was our **first year as a married couple** — and what a way to begin. We exchanged vows in Hawaii, honeymooned halfway around the world, and came home to a house full of projects and a year of growth.

## January — saying goodbye, and getting ready

We welcomed the new year with dinner at Raccolto and fireworks over West Seattle. Derek took a ski trip in Nevada. It was also a tender month: **Derek's Nana passed away.** And in between, it was wedding planning, wedding details, wedding *everything* — right down to cutting down the apple tree in the front yard.

## February — we got married! 🌺

The big one: **Hannah and Derek got married in Hawaii.** Back home, we celebrated Hannah's **30th birthday** with dinner at Canlis, saw *Hamilton* at the Paramount, revisited our first-date spot at Nana's, caught Lane 8 in concert, and started demo on the upstairs bedroom wall to build a closet.

## March — honeymoon, part one: Japan

We sent Dottie to grandma and grandpa's for a two-month stay and set off on our honeymoon. First stop: **Japan** — Tokyo, Kyoto, Kōyasan, Hakone, and Osaka, where Derek turned 30. (Between trips, Derek also rewired the upstairs knob-and-tube wiring properly — romance comes in many forms.)

## April — honeymoon, part two: New Zealand

From Japan we flew to **New Zealand**, road-tripping the North and South Islands and visiting Hobbiton. On the way home we squeezed in a one-day layover in Hawaii — Adela's for our favorite pasta and the North Shore for sunset.

## May — anniversary and home

We celebrated our **two-year anniversary** cooking salmon and asparagus risotto at home, Hannah picked Dottie back up from Georgia, Derek ran a garage sale for the big West Seattle Yard Sale, and Hannah discovered she loves Pilates.

## June — new goals, new job

Hannah started training for a half marathon and Derek for a full marathon in November. We bought used gym equipment for the garage, traveled to Denver for Pfriem's wedding, marathoned *Star Wars* I–VI, hosted Ruben and Myrnie for pizza — and **Derek landed a new job!**

## July — a full Seattle summer

Fourth of July kayaking, a new sprinkler system to rehab the backyard grass, the Empire Strips Back burlesque show, the aquarium, West Seattle Summer Fest and the bike ride (Derek bought Hannah a bike!), yoga at Volunteer Park, a PCT hike with Dottie, finishing the drywall in the new upstairs closet, and Hannah's first sourdough starter.
