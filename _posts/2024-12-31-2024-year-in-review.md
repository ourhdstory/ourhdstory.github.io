## The Year We Got Married (And So Much More)

2024 was the year that changed everything. It's hard to capture all the emotions, adventures, and milestones in a single post, but we're going to try.

## The Wedding

The year started with wedding planning in full swing, and in the spring, we said "I do." It was the most meaningful day of our lives — surrounded by family, friends, and so much love. We were nervous, excited, and completely present for every moment.

The whole process taught us about ourselves, our values, and what truly matters.

## Our Honeymoons

Rather than one trip, we decided to take two honeymoons to two dream destinations:

**Japan**: A magical journey through temples, gardens, and incredible food. We experienced Japanese culture, hospitality, and beauty in ways we never expected.

**New Zealand**: Adventure and nature at every turn. Mountains, fjords, adventure activities, and some of the most stunning landscapes on Earth.

These trips weren't just vacations — they were our first major adventures as a married couple and helped us understand how we like to travel together.

## Coming Home

After the adventures, we focused on building our home life. We:
- Started planning our garden
- Got our first chickens
- Invested in home improvements
- Built routines together
- Created traditions

## The Learning Curve

Marriage, homeownership, and raising animals all came with learning curves. We made mistakes, we laughed at ourselves, we problem-solved together. Every challenge made our relationship stronger.

## The Surprise of Contentment

After the excitement of the wedding and travels, we expected a letdown. Instead, we discovered the deep satisfaction of building a life together — the quiet mornings, the projects we tackle as a team, the simple evenings at home.

## Gratitude

We're grateful for:
- Each other (obviously)
- Family and friends who celebrated with us
- The ability to travel and explore
- A home to make our own
- Health and happiness
- New experiences and challenges

## Looking to 2025

As we close out 2024, we're excited about what's ahead. More projects, more adventures (both far and near), and continuing to build our story together.

Thank you to everyone who's been part of our journey this year. Here's to a 2025 filled with growth, adventure, and continued happiness.

**This year, we didn't just plan a wedding — we started a life together. And it's been more beautiful than we imagined.**
